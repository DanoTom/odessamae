## Descripción del canal (885 de 1.000 caracteres)

Las primeras líneas son las que aparecen en búsquedas, por eso concentran género, época y formato.

```text
Southern soul ballads and Memphis soul from 1962, told as one woman's story, one song at a time.

Odessa Mae sings about the long way home, two coffee cups on a kitchen table, letters from Chicago that stopped coming and a porch light she couldn't turn off. Every song is a new chapter of her life: vintage 1960s soul, deep soul and soul blues with warm analog sound, horns, Hammond organ and a voice that tells the truth.

New chapter every Tuesday and Friday. Start with Chapter 1 in the playlist "The Odessa Mae Story - In Order".

For late nights, slow dances, rainy afternoons and anyone who loves classic 60s soul.

About this project: Odessa Mae is a fictional singer created with AI. Music and vocals are generated with Suno; lyrics are written with AI assistance and edited by [pen name]. Images are AI-generated in a 1960s style. None of this is a real historical recording.
```
